# Evidence of Relevance: User Interviews

## 1. Purpose

The purpose of this activity is to validate the relevance of the proposed Intelligent GIS-Based Urban Services Platform for Astana by gathering feedback from representatives of its target users.

The interviews will explore how people currently report urban problems, search for lost items, obtain parking information, and interact with city services. The findings will be used to distinguish verified needs from assumptions and to refine the project requirements.

## 2. Research Objectives

The interviews aim to:

- Identify problems users experience when interacting with urban services.
- Understand how users currently report hazards or infrastructure issues.
- Explore difficulties related to finding lost items.
- Understand how users obtain information about parking availability.
- Identify expectations regarding a unified GIS-based platform.
- Determine which proposed features are most valuable to target users.

## 3. Target Participants

The planned research includes 3–5 discussions with representatives of relevant user groups.

Potential participant groups include:

- Astana residents who use city services.
- People who have reported urban hazards or infrastructure problems.
- People who have searched for lost belongings.
- Drivers who regularly look for parking.
- Representatives of organizations responsible for urban services, if accessible.

Participants should be selected according to their experience with the problems being studied. The report should document participant roles without including personal information that they have not agreed to share.

## 4. Interview Questions

### Current Urban Services

1. How do you currently report a road hazard, damaged infrastructure, or another urban problem?
2. What difficulties do you experience when contacting city services?
3. How do you usually find out whether a reported problem has been addressed?

### Lost Items

4. If you lose an item in Astana, how do you try to find it?
5. What makes it difficult to connect a lost-item report with an item that someone has found?
6. Would a service that suggests potentially matching lost-item reports be useful to you? Why or why not?

### Parking Information

7. How do you currently find parking near your destination?
8. What problems do you experience when looking for an available parking space?
9. Would short-term parking availability predictions be useful for your trips? Under what conditions?

### Proposed Platform

10. Would you use one map-based platform for several urban services, or would separate services be preferable?
11. Which of the proposed features would be most useful to you, and why?
12. What concerns would you have about the accuracy, privacy, or reliability of such a platform?
13. What would make you trust the information provided by the platform?

Interviewers may ask follow-up questions to clarify an answer. The same core questions should be used consistently where practical.

## 9. Limitations

The results will be limited by the number and backgrounds of participants. A sample of 3–5 people can provide useful exploratory feedback but cannot represent the views of all residents of Astana.

Interview responses may also be affected by participants' past experiences, recall, and personal preferences. The report should describe who participated, how the interviews were conducted, and any important limitations.

If the interviews have not yet been completed, this document represents the research plan and recording template, not evidence that the project's relevance has already been validated.

## 10. Conclusion

The interviews are intended to provide initial evidence about target users' needs and the relevance of the proposed urban-service platform. Their results will be used to distinguish assumptions from verified findings and to refine project requirements.

The final conclusion will be written after the planned discussions have been completed and their results have been analyzed.
